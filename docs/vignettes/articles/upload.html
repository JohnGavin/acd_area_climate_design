Lookup: upload addresses → ACD codes

Bulk address resolution against Vienna’s ADRESSENOGD

Quick guide

Upload an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file with at least three columns: postcode (PLZ), street name, and house number. The lookup runs entirely in your browser using DuckDB-Wasm — your file never leaves your device.

Step What to do
1 Click Choose file (or drag-and-drop)
2 Map your columns to PLZ / Street / HouseNumber if the auto-detect is wrong
3 Click Run lookup
4 Review results and click Download CSV

Example values: postcode 1210, street Achengasse, house number 3 → ACD code 070268, SCD 00012.

Coverage: 291,823 registered address points, 196,142 unique ACD codes. Source: Vienna Open Government Data WFS (ogdwien:ADRESSENOGD), snapshot 2026-05-02.

Privacy: addresses you upload are processed locally in your browser via DuckDB-Wasm. They are never sent to any server.

Methodology

The lookup performs an exact string join on three normalised keys:

  • PLZ — four-digit postcode (cast to VARCHAR)
  • Street name — LOWER(TRIM(name_str)) matched against the user column
  • House number — LOWER(TRIM(name_onr)) matched against the user column

Schrebergärten / garden-plot addresses with a / in the street or Parz in the house-number column are split into two lookup attempts automatically.

For full methodology, including the confidence-tier model and spatial enrichment from the building layer, see the Methodology page.
