Key insight:
  ACD values are 6-digit zero-padded integers ranging 1 to 232762.
  Zone OBJECTID range: 63576 to 63991 (no overlap with ACD range).
  Zone GEBID range: 1 to ~99 (2-digit max, no overlap with ACD pattern).
  ERPLABEL format is district/seq/version (e.g. 3/004/1) - no numerical overlap.
  ACD appears to be a building registry number (Adress-Code / Gebaeudekennzahl),
  NOT a zone reference field. Each building has a unique ACD value.
  This means the acd_agreement logic is WRONG: interpreting non-empty ACD as
  city saying the building is in a zone is a category error.
